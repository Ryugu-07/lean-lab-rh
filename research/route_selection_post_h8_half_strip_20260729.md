## Primary-source reading

Endres--Steiner study

```text
H_BK = -i (x d/dx + 1/2)
```

on `L^2(R_{>},dx)`. The formal eigenmode at real energy `E` is proportional to
`x^(-1/2+iE)`. Its squared modulus is `1/x`, so it is not square-integrable on the positive
half-line. The source proves the corresponding self-adjoint realization has purely continuous
spectrum. It then studies compact quantum graphs, where discrete spectra exist but have the
wrong Weyl growth to equal the Riemann-zero spectrum.

Connes--Consani identify the same symmetrized `PQ` expression with unitary scaling and explain
that its uncut spectrum is not discrete. Their comparison also prevents a false merge: matching
semiclassical emission and absorption counts does not make the Berry--Keating and Connes quantum
models equivalent.

- <https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.3183>
- <https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.14368>

## Omission reading

The historical failures do not eliminate every scaling-Hamiltonian route. They eliminate two
specific naive repairs:

1. the unconfined positive half-line does not turn the generalized modes into `L^2` eigenvectors;
2. a fixed compact graph has the wrong high-energy counting law.

An overlooked viable repair would therefore need a global arithmetic quotient, noncompact
confinement with discrete spectral data, an energy-dependent boundary/cutoff, or an absorption
trace mechanism. It must also reproduce multiplicity and the `T log T` leading count, not merely
the first few ordinates.
