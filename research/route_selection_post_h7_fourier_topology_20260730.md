## Primary-source audit

Primary sources:

- James Maynard and Kyle Pratt, *Half-isolated zeros and zero-density estimates*, Appendix C,
  Lemma 23: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.11729>.
- Norman Levinson and Hugh L. Montgomery,
  *Zeros of the derivatives of the Riemann zeta-function*, Theorem 1 and Section 2:
  <https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/117/6174-11511_2006_Article_BF02392141.pdf>.
- G. H. Hardy and J. E. Littlewood, *The zeros of Riemann's zeta-function on the critical
  line*, Lemmas 2--4:
  <https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/download/pdf/PPN266833020_0010/LOG_0029.pdf>.

Maynard--Pratt choose `M = 2*T^(1/100)` and `Y = T^(1/2)`. Their coefficient gap gives
`a(1)=1` and `a(n)=0` for `2 <= n <= M`. They truncate above
`Y*(log T)^2/2`, split the remaining finite interval into powers-of-two blocks, and compare it
with the shifted Mellin identity. Small head, far tail, and retained residue errors then force
one dyadic block to have size at least `1/(3*log T)` or the shifted integral to have size at
least `1/3`.

The project already proves the coefficient gap, actual smoothed series, inverse Mellin theorem,
one-pole contour shift, shifted-line integrability, retained residue, and the source head/tail
identity. It also proves only a source-independent finite detector. The missing source content
is the actual tail truncation, dyadic partition and cardinality, residue budget, and their
simultaneous specialization.
