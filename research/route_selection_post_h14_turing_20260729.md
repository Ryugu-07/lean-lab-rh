## Source-locked omission

Brian Conrey's 2024 paper defines

```text
f_q(x) = sum_{n>=1} chi_q(n) * sin(2*pi*n*x) / n^2.
```

Proposition 1 says that `f_q(x)=0` forces `x` rational. Its proof uses Corollary 1 and treats

```text
integer - integer/(q*x)
```

as rational only when `x` is rational. The already compiled audit shows that this inference has
an omitted zero-numerator branch. The paper later states that branch explicitly and calls the
resulting flat interval unlikely, but does not exclude it there.

The source context matters. Corollary 1 assumes `q>3`, squarefree, and `q congruent to 3 mod 8`.
The candidate `q=7` satisfies `q congruent to 7 mod 8`, not the RH-imitation condition. Therefore
an actual `q=7` flat interval would prove that the branch is real in the adjacent genuine
quadratic-character family, but it would not refute the contextually scoped Proposition 1 or the
main RH implication.
